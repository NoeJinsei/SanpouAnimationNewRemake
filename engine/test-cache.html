data-cache layer test

The same tree is painted twice: once plain, once with the busy group marked data-cache="busy" data-box="0 0 900 640", at a whole-pixel position. The timing shows what the cache saves.
Equality: shapes, positions and edges come out the same; channel values may still differ by a few levels, because (1) translucent shapes composited inside the bitmap and then over the background round differently from compositing them one by one (source-over is associative, 8-bit rounding is not), (2) gradient dithering depends on the surface, and (3) Chrome's software rasteriser anti-aliases text on a transparent surface differently. The test passes when no channel differs by more than 3 on a GPU browser (see the numbers). ?nograd=1 removes the gradient, ?blur=1 also paints a blurred, multiplied cached group on the right (visual check only).
