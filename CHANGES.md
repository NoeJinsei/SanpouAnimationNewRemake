# Changes from the original, by scene

One row per decision: what the original has, what the remake has, why. The owner approves these on a style
frame. At most five rows per round, so each can be judged. Status: proposed / approved / dropped.

## Scene 01 入荷 (and the shared world: sky, city, street, truck) — round 1, proposed

| # | Element | Original | Remake | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clouds | Seven small puffs, four lobes each, one grey-blue underside, all one size class | Fewer, larger composed cumulus: many lobes on a flat base, pure white with one crisp shade edge on the side away from the sun; a big slow bank far back and a few nearer ones | The reference's sky is its strongest part; big white shapes also give the frame a clear light-dark design |
| 2 | Light and shadow | A sun disc with a soft glow; buildings have one thin shade strip | One sun for everything: up to four broad light shafts in the sky, a lit side and a shade side on every block, flat diagonal cast shadows on the big sunlit walls, a contact shadow under everything | Lighting is what makes flat art look expensive; today the scene has no light direction |
| 3 | Depth | Three city tiers with haze, all similar in strength | Clear steps: far tiers paler and simpler, the street stronger and more saturated, the darkest values kept for the road, the cab and the outline | So the eye lands on the truck and the dock first, as in the reference's subject-over-silhouette build |
| 4 | Working detail | Buildings and truck are clean but plain: flat walls, blank roof boxes, bare road | Three levels of detail on things with a job: supplier building (drain pipes, roof units with fans, vents, dock bumpers and leveller, safety stripes, a pallet and a roll cage by the door, door lamp), truck (panel seams, hinges, mirror, mud flaps, tail lamps, reflectors, tyre detail), street (kerb joints, manhole, road studs, guard rail) | "Detail that implies a working machine" is what the owner liked in the reference |
| 5 | Shade colour | Shades are translucent navy at one strength | Three tones per surface, shade pushed toward navy blue, white things shading into periwinkle | Consistent, clean colour in every scene; avoids the grey, muddy look |

Not changed in this round: sky colours (the July family stays), the sun's place, building shapes and places,
the truck's design and lettering, Tokyo Tower, timings and motion.
