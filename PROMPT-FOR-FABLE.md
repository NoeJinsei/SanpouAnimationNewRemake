**The motion pass, across everything.** Thirty-frame design timeline drawn at up to 60Hz. Every value a pure function of the frame or the ambient clock — never `Date.now()`, never a CSS animation or transition inside the art, because the player freezes its clocks for 160ms after any input and anything on its own timeline will jump. Arrivals decelerate with a long soft landing, departures accelerate, heavy things show weight, nothing pops or switches pose in one frame, and the world is never fully frozen — every ambient element gets its own slow phase, small, never competing with the action.

One boundary that has bitten us before: springy overshoot, idle floating and inconsistent easing are on my list of old problems. **Inside the illustration**, weight and a small settle are what I'm asking for. **In the hero's interface** — the overlay copy, the buttons, the progress indicator, anything that is chrome rather than picture — it is transform and opacity only, no overshoot, no idle float, and one ease, `cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1)`, the same curve the stage dissolve uses.

**Hard engine rules. Breaking these breaks the live site.**

- The node list a scene returns must keep the **same shape every frame**. The renderer diffs by position, so a conditional null in the middle of a list shifts every sibling after it. Switch things off with a constant null in a fixed slot or with opacity 0 — never with a condition that sometimes vanishes. There are live hazards already: the plane's child list changes between phases and its whole group disappears for part of its cycle, and the finale drops a child near the end of the dissolve.
- **SVG ids must be unique per scene** — two stages coexist during a dissolve. Prefix every new gradient and clip path with its scene. The finale's `haloSoft`, `sunGlow` and `horizHaze`, and the warehouse's own ids, aren't namespaced today; fix that before adding more.
- **Allowed drawing only:** flat fills, hard edges, the `h()` tag vocabulary in `STYLE.md` §2. No filters, no blur, no masks, no images, no `foreignObject`, no `use`, no patterns, no texture, no new library. Soft gradients in exactly four places: the sky, the sun's glow, the haze between tiers, and one pale halo behind a scene's subject. The map's two masks, the warehouse's warm gradients and the vignette are existing breaches to fix, not to copy.
- **The renderer is still an open question, so ask me.** `engine/vcanvas.js` draws the same scene code on a canvas and matches the SVG output but measured no faster; the recommendation on the table is to stay SVG/DOM and keep canvas as a fallback, and I haven't answered. Until I do, assume SVG/DOM — and keep every new shape inside the canvas engine's vocabulary anyway, because anything outside it silently disappears on that side.
- **One light for the whole film:** sun top right, lit sides face right, shade sides face left, cast shadows fall down-left as flat shapes and step whatever they cross exactly one tone down. Shade is the base pushed toward navy, never toward grey or black; white things shade into periwinkle.
- **Sized for the phone:** the stage shows at about two thirds and cropped, so nothing that matters is thinner than 3 stage px, and leave out rows of tiny repeated marks.
- **The copy overlay** covers roughly the left 60% with a pale wash and the bottom with a dark band. Put the action and the good detail where it's actually seen, and keep nothing dark or busy under the headline. **Copy contrast is WCAG AA over every frame, measured and not estimated** — the last run over 209 samples came back headline 7.7:1, body 8.7:1, scene caption 5.1:1, and the unfilled progress track needs 3:1 over any part of the art. Anything you repaint behind the copy gets measured again before you call it done.
- **Memoised drawings:** almost every big element is built once and cached, so anything that moves must live *outside* the cache. Cached groups carry a `data-box` — if the geometry changes, the box changes with it or the drawing clips.
- **Batch** small repeated shapes into one path per colour and round coordinates to one decimal. **The render budget has a number:** the target is under about 1 ms of script per frame — the original measures 0.44–0.95 ms in the site's own measurement — and we are already over it in this project's viewer, where round 2's scene 01 draws at 1.98 ms against the original's 2.61 ms and the city rebuild at 1.97 against 2.57. So the honest test is not the absolute figure, it's the same-sitting comparison against `original`: a pass must not be meaningfully slower than the side it replaces, and getting back toward 1 ms is the direction.
- **Watch the frame-rate trap:** if the smoothed render cost goes over 15ms the player silently drops the whole film to 30fps, and only recovers below 9ms. A too-rich remake makes the animation worse, not better. Measure, don't guess.
- **Edit through part files**, never by hand-editing the big file: `round2/parts/<name>.js` with exact `//== REPLACE from "..." to "..."` anchors, applied with `python tools/splice.py round2/js/hero-port-b.js round2/parts/<part>.js`, then `node --check` the result. The anchors are exact text and are consumed by the first splice, so a sample side is always built from a **fresh copy** of `round2/js` by its own `build.sh`. One part file per element, in `round2/parts/`, and a sample side's `build.sh` points at *that* file — don't make a second copy under `samples/`, which is exactly the trap `city-v2` is in today. Add each new side to `sides.json` with `"schedule": {"holdAmbient02": true}`, or the viewer's clock won't match it.
- Don't shadow the palette object `DAY` with a local variable — two functions already use `L` for something else.
- **Never touch** `C:\Users\RTX4070TI\Desktop\sanpou-site-v2`. Read it only. Integration is a separate task after approval, and website wording is a separate task too. **Never touch `original/` either** — it is the baseline, and `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` has to keep passing.
- Before installing **any** skill, plugin or library, run the `skill-inspector` skill (`skillspector scan <folder> --no-llm`) and show me the verdict first. Ask me before adding a library. Save any link I give you into `REFERENCES.md`.
