The four fixes on the batch

The batch was accepted; these four things were fixed afterwards, one commit each. Every picture shows before and after, at the desktop framing and at what a phone sees of that scene.

Fix 1The plane is no longer sliced by Tokyo Tower

It crossed behind the Tower's lattice, which cut through the fuselage and the windows. Three options were built and judged on a phone. Picked: A, a steeper climb (43 degrees) that passes in front of the Tower's thin mast, above the top deck, so nothing is drawn across the plane. It also removes 68 nodes.

Fix 2The map's background is alive where a phone looks

In a second and a half not one pixel changed in the left third, and the moving water lay outside what a phone sees. Now the Sea of Japan coast off Niigata has the same moving water, and a prefecture steps softly to white while its truck stands at its destination. Pixels that change in 1.5 s inside the phone's strip: 10.7 % before, 40.6 % after; in the left third: 0 % before, 2.1 % after.

Fix 3東京 and 埼玉 are no longer the smallest names

They were 11.5 units, about 12 px on a phone, while every other name was 14 or 15. They are 15 now, each inside its own shape, in the same colour.

Fix 4The knot at the centre of the map is opened

入間 now stands on its real point beside 志木. The two place dots and the stub line are gone: the depots mark the places. The Saitama road passes behind 入間 and its truck runs all the way to its dot.

All five scenes, as the site really shows them

1. The clouds, every scene

Each cloud is now one silhouette, two to three times wider than tall, with one pale shade area on the side away from the sun. The pile of discs with a crescent on every lobe is gone.

2. The map (scene 04): background, colours, what moves

Four directions were built as option frames and judged at the real desktop and phone crops. Picked: 1, open water with the service area in Sanpou white. It is the only one that keeps the film's blue and white while giving the map real ground. What moves is the water: a band of shallow water leaves the Pacific coast and travels outward.

3. The routes: dashed lines and a small truck on each

Vermilion dashes move outward from the head office on the white casing. Each corridor has its own small truck, designed at map size (about 19 px wide on a phone). It leaves once its corridor has drawn, arrives softly, and the run loops, so two to five are always on the road.

4. The trucks (scenes 01, 02, 03)

Redrawn from a blank page as a modern cab-over box truck. 三豊運輸 is the big line on the box (about 27 px on a phone) and SANPOU the small one under it.

The loading at both docks, with the new truck

5. The airport, the terminal, the plane and the rest of scene 05

The plane is a proper twin-jet with a neutral livery, and it now climbs through open sky: the finale's skyline is composed again under its climb line, and at every measured moment it is over no building. Since fix 1 it passes in front of Tokyo Tower's thin mast instead of behind its lattice. The Sanpou depot carries 三豊運輸 on a navy band, where a phone can read it. The airport has a real control tower, a terminal under one rising roof, an apron with ground vehicles and an edge-on runway.

The value range

The nearest city row is now clearly navy, the row behind it is no longer washed out, and the far rows are paler: five steps from navy to sky. The sky, the clouds and the whites are untouched.

For you to decide

  • The plane's climb is 43 degrees, steep for an airliner, and on desktop it sits right at the Tower's tip for a moment at 40.0 s (a close pass in front of the mast). On a phone it goes up behind the buttons before reaching the Tower.
  • 入間's depot arrives at 1.8 times its size instead of 1.9, so that it does not touch 志木 as it lands. The arrival was a closed item; this is the one number of it that changed.
  • The left third of the map is alive only at its right edge (the water off Niigata and Toyama Bay), and the prefectures' answer to their trucks is soft, because most of them are nearly white already.
  • The depot's name is vertical (三豊運輸 written downward on a navy band). That is what keeps it inside the strip a phone sees and under the plane's climb. A horizontal sign would be smaller or cut off on a phone.
  • The map's prefecture names are a mid navy and turn pale under the site's wash at the top of the frame (新潟, 栃木). They can go a step darker if you want.
  • On a phone the map's moving water shows only as a thin strip at the right edge, because the map fills the phone. The trucks carry the motion there.
  • Scene 02 on a phone cuts the name on both trucks in view (豊運輸 on one, 三豊 on the next). That is where the trucks stand, which is closed.
  • The truck has no roof deflector now, so nothing stands in front of the box.
  • Scene 02's sky is sparse in the middle: it shares its clouds with scene 01, where a cloud there would cover the sun.
  • Still the original drawings: scene 03's expressway, lamps, gantry, signs and fence; the street, lamps, vending machines and foreground rail of scenes 01 and 02. Scene 03's caption still says 関東一円; copy is yours to decide.
