1. The trucks on the map drive; they no longer slide

The truck was drawn from the side on a map drawn from above, mirrored once per corridor and never turned, and five of the seven corridors run more north than east. It is now seen from above and steered along its road: the cab leads, and the tail swings through a bend. Three directions were built and judged on a phone.

No corridor passes under a name card

Two did (Gunma's and Niigata's, behind 志木営業所). Niigata's now rounds the card on the east and Tochigi's moved over for it. Gunma's shares Saitama's road as far as the gap between the two cards and branches north there: that gap has room for one corridor only. Every corridor was sampled against every card: none is under one.

2. The plane is on a phone for four seconds

A phone's only open sky in the finale is the band between the two buttons and the roofs, left of Tokyo Tower. The plane crossed it in about a second, because it kept gathering speed all the way up. The path past the Tower's mast is the one you accepted; only the speed along it changed. It now climbs at a steady speed, close to its approach speed, and gathers speed again once its nose has gone up behind the buttons on a phone.

3. The arrival is shown at the place

A whole prefecture stepped to white while its truck stood there. Most of the area is nearly white already, so it only made the service area look patchy at different moments. That step is gone: every prefecture keeps its own tint all the time. Instead the place dot at the corridor's end opens into a ring as the truck comes to rest, stays while it stands and closes as the truck goes.

4. The last old drawings: scene 03's road

Everything the expressway is made of was still the original drawing. All of it is new: the gantry is an open steel truss, the two green signs keep their words and are larger and narrower so a phone shows them whole, the road has a concrete parapet with a rail, two lanes, a hard shoulder and a guard rail in front, and the lamps are tapered columns with a swept arm. The caption was not touched.

4. The last old drawings: the street of scenes 01 and 02

The street was still the round 2 drawing. All of it is new: the paving, kerb and gutter, the road and its dashes (placed so the site's caption stands over a gap), the lamps, the utility poles and their lines, the vending corner, and a pedestrian fence of pipe hoops in the foreground. There are three lamps where there were eleven: the other eight stood behind the two buildings and were never seen.

For you to decide

  • Gunma's corridor shares Saitama's road as far as the gap between the 志木 and 入間 cards and branches north there. It was the only way through without moving a card.
  • The plane's climb is slow, about half a plane-length a second, like its approach. If it reads as floating, one number makes it faster and costs time on a phone (the options picture shows 3.1 s and 4.9 s beside the picked 4.1 s).
  • The plane passes the Tower after 45.8 s, while the finale holds. A visitor who stays sees it; the cycle is 26 s now (was 19).
  • Kanagawa's place dot already sat on the last stroke of 川; the ring now opens around that stroke. Clearing it means moving the dot or the name, and both are closed.
  • The service area: the pale steps it has now, or one flat white (the extra picture in section 3).
  • Three lamps in scenes 01 and 02 where there were eleven. Say if you want more in the open stretch between the two buildings.
  • The power lines still cross Tokyo Tower in scene 01, as they did before.
  • Scene 03 on a phone: the big board is whole inside the strip for about 0.3 s as it passes. That comes from the scroll and the camera, which are closed. The lamps are faint there, because nothing above the barrier may get darker.
  • Scene 03's caption still says 関東一円. It was not touched; it is your call.
