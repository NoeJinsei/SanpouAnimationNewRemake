# The headline is already an open question to the client — F-2 (2026-11-19)

Found in `sanpou-site-v2/docs/CLIENT-QUESTIONS-ja.md`, which I should have read a week ago.

## The line on the site was never approved

**F-2** records that the headline now on the site —

> 関東1都6県と新潟市方面へ、／板橋からお届けします。

— is **the build team's proposal, not the client's text, and it is explicitly marked
「ご承認をいただくまでの案です」: a placeholder standing until the client approves it.**

So the line the owner has been dissatisfied with for a week is not a finished decision anybody defended.
It is an open question with a number, waiting for an answer.

## And the client's own original said what round H arrived at

The client's own headline, which F-2 proposed replacing, was:

> 板橋から、**運ぶ・保管する**。／関東1都6県と新潟市方面へ。

The build team's reason for changing it was narrow and reasonable: 「板橋から」 does not connect naturally to
「保管する」. They did not reject the idea — only the join.

**That idea is exactly where round H landed, independently.** The pick was
**「荷を運ぶ、荷を預かる。」** — carry and keep, in two matched beats. Eight rounds of work, a 90-hero survey
and a verification pass converged on the thing the client wanted to say in the first place, with the join
fixed: two parallel clauses instead of one place-phrase straining to govern both verbs.

That is a much better thing to put in front of the client than "we think your line is wrong".

## What this changes about how the headline work should be handled

Everything from rounds A to H belongs in **F-2 as proposals to the client**, not as an edit to the HTML.
`COPY-STYLE-ja.md` §0 says exactly this: wording proposals go to `CLIENT-QUESTIONS-ja.md`, and F-4 shows
the form — the client's sentence is left in place verbatim and the improvement is asked for, with the
reason, and the page is not touched until they answer.

**Two further corrections to things I said earlier:**

1. The E1, F2 and F3 headlines I proposed were verbless place-to-place lines. Not only does
   `COPY-STYLE-ja.md` ban that shape — the November wording pass had **already removed exactly that shape**
   from scene captions 03 and 04 of the animation, with the reason recorded as "the verbless place＋へ shape
   copy-rules bans". I proposed putting back into the headline what had just been taken out of the captions.
2. The sentence I praised as the best on the site — "safety is not left to the individual driver's
   attention; it is built up as a company system" — **is the client's own text**, and F-4 ① already proposes
   a rewrite of it, held until they approve. I had no idea whose sentence I was admiring.
