## The one thing they started and did not finish — which today's cuts completed

This resolves what looked like a conflict. The project's fact list bans 「当日・緊急スポット便」, but the
October file appears at first glance to keep emergency work: the client left 「緊急のスポット便」 inside one
paragraph and 「催事・イベント物資の緊急輸送 など」 in an example list.

**They are not a contradiction. They are leftovers.** The client *did* delete the service — they emptied the
heading 「チャーター・緊急スポット便」 outright — but missed two mentions buried in prose, and those two
carried forward into the new site. They are exactly the two lines cut on 2026-11-19 (`d73a789`, `132fa77`)
after the owner confirmed the service is not offered.

So the owner's "No, cut it" and the client's October intent agree, and the site now matches both.
