三豊運輸 hero remake: the film, end to end

The whole film in one take, with no cuts: 48 seconds, from the first frame to after the plane has passed Tokyo Tower. The film itself is not changed by this page. It is only here so the film can be watched.

Recorded again on 16 November after the birds and the sky were redone (what changed, with before and after). All four were recorded again, at a higher picture quality than the first time, so the skies stay smooth in the recording.

The remake's two were recorded again on 17 November, after the overlay changed a second time. On the remake the hero's title is up during the opening scene and then clears, so the film plays in the whole frame, and it comes back when it is paused or reached by keyboard (what changed, with before and after). The wash behind it is no longer a pale band across half the screen: it is a soft patch behind the copy block only, at the faintest strength that still measures legible, and it drifts away as the copy clears (the strengths, measured). On a phone the copy block is about 110 px shorter, so the film starts higher. The original keeps the overlay the site has today - that is what it is here to show. So each pair holds both changes at once: the film itself, and how much of it a visitor is allowed to see.

The remake's two were recorded once more late on 17 November, after round 11 (what changed, with before and after): the last scene is now a loop, the first truck drives in from outside the picture, the trucks' rear doors are shut while a scene rests, and nothing loses speed in one frame as a scene comes to rest. They also show the overlay as it stands since 17 November. The original's two are the recordings of 16 November: the original has not changed. This recording ends at 48 s; the last scene's first full turn ends at 82 s, so two loops through are on the round 11 page.

The remake's two were recorded again on 18 November, after round 12 (what changed, with before and after): in the last scene the clouds go a little further and faster, and the plane gathers speed from its roll all the way up the climb. It stands on the runway a little longer first, and passes Tokyo Tower at the same moment as before. The original's two are unchanged.

Press play on the first one and watch it through. The original is under it, and the same two on a phone are further down. "Play both together" starts a remake and its original from the beginning at the same moment.

  • How they were made: node tools/record.mjs <side> <file> --framing=desktop|phone --from=0 --to=48000 --fps=60 --crf=18, the phone ones with --dpr=2. The sides are round2 (the remake as it stands, with the birds and the sky redone and the copy clearing after the opening) and original. If the film or the overlay changes again, record them again.
  • What a recording is: every frame is drawn for its own moment of the site's clock and saved, one after the other, so the file shows the film exactly as it is meant to play at 60 frames a second. It includes the site's own copy, buttons, dark band and progress bar, as the site lays them over the film.
  • What it is not: a measure of how smoothly a given computer or phone plays the film live. That was measured separately (the cost section of the round 7 page).
  • Jumping about: this page loads each film whole before it plays, so its bar can be dragged to any second. (The viewer's server cannot send part of a file, and a film played straight from it can only run from the start.)
  • The picture is the site's hero section itself: 1440 x 720 in a desktop window of 1440 x 900, and 390 x 680 on a phone of 390 x 844.
  • How "none dropped" was checked: sh tools/check-recording.sh <file.mp4> - each file holds 2,880 frames, which is 48 seconds at 60 a second; every frame comes exactly 1/60 s after the one before; and no frame's picture repeats the one before it, which is what a dropped frame would look like. All four pass.

What changed and why is on the review pages: round 12: faster clouds, a plane that builds speed, round 11: the loop and what was not smooth, the birds and the sky, the three fixes, round 7.
