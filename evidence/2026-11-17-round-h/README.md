# Round H — the wording, with every phrase verified before it was ranked (2026-11-17)

Five rounds were rejected and two native-ear audits found one cause: **every line had been built by
INVENTING a phrase.** 角をつぶさない, 受け取る手で, 同じ屋根の下の棚と荷台, 荷物を整える, また朝が来て — not
one of those pairings exists in Japanese. So round H was run with a stage the earlier rounds did not have:
**every candidate listed its own word pairings, and a verification pass searched for each one in real
Japanese before anything was ranked.**

It worked. **36 candidates were verified; 10 came back doubtful or invented and were killed before the
ranking even saw them.** That is the check that would have caught all five earlier rounds.

The options are in `copy/hero-copy-h.json`; `desktop.jpg` and `phone.jpg` show them in the real hero.

## What the verification actually found

- **荷を預かる is real and is this trade's own phrase** (~2,530 exact-phrase hits): a warehousing BCP case
  study for 兵機海運 (「倉庫業を行う企業として荷主から荷を預かる以上…」), 中京倉庫's own recruiting page,
  a cold-storage executive in 水産タイムス, CBRE on bayside warehousing, a 豊洲 wholesaler. Note the
  register test that came with it: **荷物を預かる (~84,600) is cloakroom and hotel-luggage language**, so
  choosing 荷 over 荷物 is the correct trade choice, not a quirk.
- **預かる appears in NONE of the 90 real heroes** in `research/jp-hero-survey-2026-11-17.md`. It is the one
  word available here that is not already on somebody else's home page.
- **The worn alternatives are worn, with names attached.** 信頼を運ぶ (~160,000) is used by at least six
  carriers — 西町運輸倉庫, 古屋運送, 鷺富運送, 田中陸運, 川崎商事運輸, 株式会社H&R — and 埼玉朝陽運輸's
  hero is literally 「安心と信頼を運ぶ」. 安心を運ぶ (~143,000) is two companies' actual headline:
  報友梱包運輸 and 桜南運輸. Any of those fails the paste test on contact.
- **物流を担う never stands bare.** ~354,000 hits and in every one it carries a possessor or qualifier —
  TOYOTAの, バンダイナムコグループの, 国際, 食品, 釜石の. No attested use with nothing holding its scale down.
- **荷を運ぶ's live habitat is porterage.** ~34,200 hits, but nine of the top ten are manual handling:
  歩荷, 仲仕, 牛方, 人力運搬, 港湾用語集 glossing 仲仕 as 「荷を運ぶ人」, and the labour ministry's own
  definition of 横持ち. A modern carrier writes 荷物を運ぶ or 貨物を輸送する.

## The five that survived

- **H1 「荷を運ぶ、荷を預かる。」** (11, one line) — **the pick.** *We carry goods. We take goods into our
  keeping.* The order is the argument: Japanese puts what matters last, so the line ends on the half a
  trucking-only firm has no right to write. Nothing in it has to be worked out — the fault that sank five
  rounds. **Against it:** 荷を運ぶ opens on the porterage register above, and on the one claim every operator
  in the country could make; the two halves are symmetrical enough to read as a construction.
- **H2 「荷を預かる物流」** (7, noun-ending, no punctuation) — the shortest, exactly the length of the
  best-written hero either reader found in the 90. The frame `[Xを]預かる物流` is live with freight objects
  (上田コールド, 近鉄ロジスティクス, 安田不動産). **Against:** at seven characters with no adverb it can land
  as naming the category rather than making a claim, and one company uses the same phrase as a plain label.
- **H3 「荷を預かる、荷を運ぶ。」** (11) — the same four words in **the film's own order**: 01 入荷, 02 積込,
  03 輸送, so the line and the scenes tell the story the same way round. **Against:** it spends the stressed
  final position on 運ぶ, so the thing that separates this company arrives first and is gone by the end.
- **H4 「荷を預かる、荷を守る。」** (11) — **the best attested line in the whole exercise**: the 兵機海運 case
  study writes both halves in one sentence, in this order, with this meaning. **Against:** the mass of real
  荷を守る usage is site safety — crane loads, 玉掛け, KY sheets — so a reader with depot experience may hear
  a wall placard. That is the fault that killed 「角ひとつ、つぶさない。」.
- **H5 「荷を守る、／物流を担う。」** (5 + 6) — the soberest, and the only survivor with no 預 in it, which
  matters because scene 01's caption 「お預かりした荷物を、丁寧に検品します。」 shares the frame. **Against:**
  bare 物流を担う over-claims, as the search above shows.
